# Malaysia Platform Notes

Use this when the business targets Malaysia.

## Platform reality
| Platform | Role in Malaysia | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Still the main buying platform for 30+ audiences, mums and households | Click-to-WhatsApp ads are the standard path for small and medium businesses |
| Instagram | Secondary, more visual/aspirational | Reels and carousels; good for premium look and UGC |
| TikTok | Fastest growing; TikTok Shop is heavily price-competitive | Great for awareness, but watch margins (see below) |
| WhatsApp | Where the sale actually closes | Treat it as the checkout. See `sms/references/whatsapp.md` |

## TikTok: organic vs TikTok Shop
TikTok **organic content** (brand videos that drive to WhatsApp or the website) does **not** have marketplace fees. The margin worry only applies to **TikTok Shop** (selling inside TikTok).

Before recommending TikTok Shop, calculate margin per order. Always check current fee rates in TikTok Seller Center, because they change often:

```
Net per order = Selling price
              − Product cost (COGS)
              − TikTok commission %  × price
              − Transaction/payment fee % × price
              − Affiliate/creator commission % × price (if using affiliates)
              − Shipping subsidy / free-shipping programme cost
              − Packaging
              − Ad spend per order (if running TikTok Shop ads)
```

Recommended order of moves for a premium brand:
1. **Organic TikTok first**: build the brand with content (fabric close-ups, making-of, room makeovers, customisation "build your set" videos), and send traffic to WhatsApp.
2. **Then TikTok Shop with entry products only** (e.g. pillowcases, the RM119 set) to protect premium margins and avoid price wars.
3. Keep fully-custom/high-ticket sets on WhatsApp, where salespeople can upsell.

## Content angles that work for premium home goods in Malaysia
- Before/after bedroom makeovers
- "Build your set" (colour + fabric + sham + comforter picker)
- ASMR fabric touch / close-ups
- Homestay/Airbnb host testimonials (B2B proof)
- Raya bedroom refresh
- Founder and family story ("perniagaan keluarga")
