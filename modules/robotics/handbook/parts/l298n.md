# L298N motor driver

Drives two DC motors, each forward, back and at any speed. ENA/ENB take PWM for speed; IN1–IN4 set direction.

## Wiring on ESP32
- Motor battery (7–12V) to **12V**, battery minus and ESP32 GND to **GND**.
- ENA/ENB to PWM-capable pins; IN1–IN4 to any output pins.
- Keep the 5V regulator jumper on below 12V; it can feed 5V sensors.

## Why it gets hot
The L298N is a bipolar bridge and wastes ~2V as heat. For small robots that is fine; for efficiency use a TB6612FNG.
