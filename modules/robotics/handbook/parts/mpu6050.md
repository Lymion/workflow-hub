# MPU6050 gyro + accelerometer

6-axis IMU on I2C (address 0x68). Use the gyro Z axis to hold a heading.

## Wiring on ESP32
SDA → GPIO 21, SCL → GPIO 22, VCC → 3V3, GND → GND.
